Privacy Policy
Last Updated: September 12, 2026

1. Overview & Business Entity

This Privacy Policy explains how Take24 ("we", "us", or "our"), operated by Helloitskortny Media LLC, collects, uses, stores, and protects user information when you access or use our workspace platform at take24.co.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information directly provided by users and via third-party integrations to deliver application services:

  • Account Information: Email address, login credentials, and user profile preferences (display name, profile avatar).
  • Creator Data: Content schedules, product sample tracking logs, video links, commission data, and custom AI script parameters.
  • TikTok API Data: When you connect your TikTok account via TikTok API integrations, we collect limited authorization tokens, account identifiers, and public video performance metrics (such as views and engagement).

3. How We Use Your Data

Core Purpose: Data collected via Take24 is strictly utilized to provide workflow automation, daily content planning, script generation, and performance analytics tailored to your creator profile.

Specific usage includes:

  • Displaying account performance metrics and content analytics in your private dashboard.
  • Generating video script concepts tailored to your chosen writing tone.
  • Syncing daily tasks, product queues, and filming schedules across your devices.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party APIs

Take24 strictly respects creator data privacy:

  • No Data Sales: We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information or API data to third-party advertisers or data brokers.
  • TikTok Integration: We store and process TikTok API data strictly in accordance with TikTok's Developer Terms and Privacy Policy. API access tokens are encrypted and used solely for user-requested application operations.
  • Service Providers: We use secure database and storage infrastructure (e.g., Supabase) to host and encrypt your private creator workspace.

5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

We retain your information as long as your Take24 account remains active. You may request account deletion or removal of third-party connected API tokens at any time through your settings or by contacting our team. Upon deletion, all associated data and tokens are permanently removed from our active database.

6. Security Measures

We employ standard security protocols, including SSL encryption in transit and database row-level security (RLS), to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

7. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact Helloitskortny Media LLC through your Account Settings page.
